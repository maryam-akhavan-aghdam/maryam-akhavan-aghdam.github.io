About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Alabama. My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, particularly their applications in healthcare.

I have an M.Sc. in Computer Science at Florida International University. I also hold an Master degree in Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence).

Research Interests

My research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Explainable AI (XAI), neuroimaging-based diagnostic AI systems, and Healthcare AI.
